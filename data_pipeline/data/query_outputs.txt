QUERY 6 - JOIN
======================================================================

SQL:
SELECT
    books.title,
    books.price_gbp,
    books.rating,
    categories.category_name
FROM books
JOIN categories
    ON books.category_id = categories.category_id
ORDER BY books.rating DESC, books.title ASC
LIMIT 10;
