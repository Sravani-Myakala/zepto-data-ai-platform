ZEPTO SUPPORT POLICY DOCUMENT

1. ORDER CANCELLATION
   Customers can cancel an order before the order is dispatched.
   Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may not be possible.
   If cancellation is unavailable, the customer should contact customer support for assistance.

2. REFUNDS
   Eligible refunds are processed after the returned or cancelled order is reviewed.
   Refund timing may depend on the payment method and the customer's bank or payment provider.
   Customers should retain their order details until the refund is completed.

3. DAMAGED OR INCORRECT ITEMS
   Customers should report damaged, missing, or incorrect items to customer support as soon as possible after delivery.
   The customer may be asked to provide photographs or other information to verify the issue.
   After verification, the support team may provide an appropriate resolution according to the applicable policy.

4. RETURN POLICY
   Return eligibility depends on the product category and condition of the item.
   Products that are damaged by the customer or used improperly may not qualify for return.
   Customers should contact support before attempting to return an item when return eligibility is unclear.

5. DELIVERY
   Delivery times shown in the application are estimates and may vary because of traffic, weather, demand, or other operational conditions.
   Customers can check the order status through the application.
   If an order is significantly delayed, customers should contact customer support.

6. PAYMENT
   Customers can use the payment methods made available during checkout.
   A payment may remain pending for some time because of bank or payment-provider processing.
   If money has been deducted but the order was not successfully created, the customer should contact support with the transaction details.

7. CUSTOMER SUPPORT
   Customers should provide their order ID when contacting customer support about an order.
   For damaged, missing, or incorrect items, customers should provide relevant details and photographs when requested.
   Support responses and resolutions depend on the specific order and applicable policy.

8. IMPORTANT NOTE
   This document is a project-specific policy knowledge base created for the Zepto Data and AI Platform support-assistant demonstration.
   The chatbot should answer questions using information retrieved from this document.
   If the document does not contain enough information to answer a question, the chatbot should clearly state that the information is not available in the policy knowledge base instead of inventing an answer.
